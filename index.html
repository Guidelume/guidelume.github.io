One On The House

THE WITCHER 3 · A MOD BY VARKORAX

One On The House

A small quality-of-life mod that smooths out a few personal frustrations with upgrade costs, alchemy supplies, and merchant funds.

What it changes

  • More affordable upgrades. Reduces the cost of Runewright investments and Corvo Bianco renovations.
  • Better-stocked alchemists. Expands the inventories of the Pellar, Novigrad’s Crematory Alchemist, and Yolar on Skellige, inspired by Toussaint’s Perfumery merchant.
  • More generous merchants. Rebalances trader funds, with an alternative that assigns fixed amounts based on each shop’s wealth class.

Configuration options let you adjust these changes or keep the original upgrade prices and merchant funds.
