A Personal Note Regarding EA

As of August 4, 2026, EA has become privately owned by a consortium led by Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, alongside Silver Lake and Affinity Partners.

This leaves me deeply uncomfortable. These games have meant a great deal to me over the years, and building this guide has been a genuine passion project. I can value the games and the work of the people who created them without overlooking the actions and policies of the company that now owns them. But I cannot ignore the serious human rights concerns surrounding this new ownership, particularly the Saudi government's treatment of LGBTQ people.

I will continue to maintain this guide and share it with the community. Doing so does not mean that I endorse EA, its new ownership, or the policies of the Saudi government.

I want to make my position clear:

I stand firmly with my LGBTQ brothers and sisters, now and always.
