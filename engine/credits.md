## Typography

Guidelume’s typefaces include Zen Dots, Quicksand, VT323, Press Start 2P, Mochiy Pop One, Lexend, and Libre Baskerville. Their letterforms are a large part of the desktop’s character, from its terminal lettering to its accessible reading options.

Font notices are included with the project: [bundled font license](./engine/fonts/OFL.txt), [Halloween font licenses](./engine/img/environment/halloween/OFL.txt), and [holiday font license](./engine/img/environment/holidays/OFL.txt).
