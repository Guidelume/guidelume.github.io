# Changelog

## 2026-10-24 · 1.0 Update | Turian Horosk

- Replaced selectable desktop modes with one interactive desktop and optional **Setup**, including local names, avatars, and an Appearance walkthrough. Guides remain available before Setup is complete.

- Refined theme and background choices, reorganized Environmental Controls, and added ambient-motion controls. Presets and seasonal themes manage their own appearance options to avoid conflicting settings.
- Reworked **Rainy Evening** and added **Halloween**, **Christmas**, and **New Year’s** scenes with themed scenery, weather, and icons, plus fireworks and a New Year’s countdown.

- Added **Documents** and **System** folders, moved **Readme** to the desktop, and added **Credits** and a locally editable **Progress-Report-01**.
- Added **Mods**, with an overview and download for **One for the Road**, a Witcher 3 mod.
- Added **Looking Glass**, a YouTube viewer with collections and personal bookmarks. It joins **The Warden’s Field Guide** prototype and the **Sound Mixer** placeholder in **System → Unfinished**.
- Added a read-only **Terminal** with a fictional text tree and playful **Blackwall** incident responses.

- Added configurable desktop **Widgets**: a timezone-aware analogue clock, a local calendar with holiday annotations, and multiple autosaving Sticky Notes with color choices and pinning above windows.

- Improved desktop scaling, window styling, taskbar overflow, and fullscreen controls. Added persistent taskbar pins, touch-friendly context menus, and optional taskbar hiding for maximized windows.

- Expanded **Backup & Restore** to version 6 with grouped previews, selective imports, and support for widgets, profiles, document edits, and Looking Glass bookmarks. Older backups remain compatible; discoveries and Setup completion stay local.
- Separated **Restore Defaults**, **Reset Discoveries**, and a complete site reset, with clearer explanations and confirmations.

- Expanded the **Developer Vault** with single-file and folder unlocks, session-only decrypted content, and cleaner media handling.
- Improved workspace restoration, saved window-focus order, and handling of inactive guides and media. First-time Nexus links open the requested guide with Setup skipped and CRT effects off.
