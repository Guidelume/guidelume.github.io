## Overview

Portal 2 was the highly anticipated sequel to the sleeper hit, you guessed it, Portal.
Originally released to pad out the Orange Box, Portal left such a strong impression that a fully fledged sequel was soon in production.

Releasing in April of 2011 it found instant acclaim for basically everything and is, in my opinion, Valve's best game to date.

It is also my favorite game of all time, so I may be slightly biased.

## Gameplay

Thanks to a slew of new additions like Gels and Lasers the game would improve a lot in complexity without turning up the difficulty too much.

The roughly 8 hour long singleplayer campaign prepared players to dive both in Co-op multiplayer, as well as the Community Testing Initiative, aka custom levels.

## Story

GLaDOS is back! After accidentally waking her up from a really, really long nap. Turns out she really was Still Alive.

Unfortunately for you, she is very angry about the whole "killing" thing.

Now your only hope of escape is a kinda slow and very british personality sphere called Wheatley.

The story is fantastic honestly and still to this day has some of the sharpest writing I have ever encountered.

## Graphics

It's Source alright, but honestly it looks damn good, even today.

There is this timeless sort of look to Source games that people more so notice with Half-Life 2, but I think it works for this game as well.

## Audio

Plenty of fun tracks to get you testing, half of which sound like they were made by sampling a dial up modem (compliment).

Ellen McLain and Stephen Merchant deliver some truly career defining performances here. I mean there is a reason people still remember GLaDOS right?

## Development

The devs have been quite open about the fact that they were a bit lost when tasked with making more Portal at first. The ending of Portal originally just faded to black after being sucked out of the facility.
On the whole though development went fairly smoothly. Once they had decided that they want to bring back Chell, the story came together quickly.

## Legacy

Portal 2 still remains one of the best rated games on Steam. It is still a landmark in narrative and puzzle games.
And Valve leaned fully into using Aperture as branding whenever they have something to announce. I think that tells you all you need to know.

## Personal Thoughts

Beyond what I've already said, I remember this game being the first time I had to upgrade my GPU. Yeah I had a really crappy PC back then, but my father knew how much I liked Portal so he got me both the game and the recommended GPU.
I've played some truly great games since then and I will hopefully, eventually, have talked about all of them here, but nothing has influenced my humor or taste in games quite as much as Portal 2.
