Milky Way Bar Crawl - Finish

Sanity Check

▸

Before finishing or continuing the crawl, it’s worth doing a quick sanity check.

  • Start a new game and get acquainted with the Mod Settings menu.
  • Play for at least 15–20 minutes.
    For example:
    • LE1: Reach Saren’s introduction on Eden Prime.
    • LE2: Make it to Jacob on Lazarus Station.
    • LE3: Get to the beacon in the harbour.
  • Spend a bit of time in a busy area (e.g. Citadel) and keep an eye on:
    • Crashes or infinite loading screens,
    • Unusual performance dips.
  • If you spot something odd, check out Common Questions first, then the comments on the Nexus guide.
    All else fails, reach out to me.

If everything looks good, consider your setup locked in. From this point onward, any additional mods are house rules: you can absolutely add them, but be prepared to do a little troubleshooting if something doesn't play nice.

Last Call

▾

Your game(s) should now be fully modded and ready for launch.

From here you’ve got two options:

  • Stop here and start playing
  • Stick around and mod your games even more.

Nothing beyond this point is required for a stable playthrough. Think of the remaining sections as another round for those wanting to go the extra mile.

If this is where we part ways: Thank you for going on the Milky Way Bar Crawl with me!
Remember: The best mod list is the one that makes you excited to start another playthrough.

See you at the next stop, Commander.

After Hours: Extras

▸

If you’d like to add a bit more flavor, the Extras section covers small, optional additions:

Note: I don’t always use these myself. They’re well-liked by the community, but you should always read each mod’s page carefully and install at your own discretion.

When you’re ready, head over to the Extras page for details.
