Milky Way Bar Crawl - Introduction

Welcome...

▾

...to the Milky Way Bar Crawl! A modding guide for the Mass Effect Legendary Edition!

Over the course of this guide we'll give all three games a comprehensive overhaul. The goal is to help you make the trilogy feel more immersive, cohesive, and alive on repeat playthroughs.

Of course, this is only one of many ways to experience the trilogy, and by no means a definitive one. I'd say modding Mass Effect is on the less complex side, especially compared to behemoths like Skyrim, but it can still feel daunting if you have never done it before. That's where this guide comes in.

?!

Obligatory Spoiler Warning:
This guide is written with the assumption that you have already finished the trilogy at least once (or several times, let's be honest) and are now looking for ways to spice up your runs.
If this is your first journey through the galaxy, I highly recommend experiencing the trilogy unmodded first. Many of the mods featured here spoil story moments simply through their names and descriptions.



Wait, who are you?
My name is Tom, but I mostly go by Varkorax. I have been playing Mass Effect since about 2012. Between the OT and the LE, I’ve completed each game well over 20 times. I started modding the OT around 2018, and have not played without mods since. So while Milky Way Bar Crawl is only about a year old, I have been refining my lists for years.

Structure

▸

Every good bar crawl needs an itinerary.

We start by getting everything ready in Setup, then visit each game in order with dedicated mod lists for each game.

Once the essentials are in place, we'll finish the evening in After Hours, where you'll find texture mods and a few optional additions for anyone looking to go a little further.

Terminology

▸

A quick primer on a few terms you'll see frequently while modding Mass Effect:

Term Full Description
OT Original Trilogy The original PC versions released between 2008 and 2012.
LE Legendary Edition The remastered version of the trilogy released in 2021.
LE1 / LE2 / LE3 Legendary Edition (per game) Shorthand for a specific LE title.
M3 ME3Tweaks Mod Manager The standard tool in the Mass Effect modding scene.
MEM Mass Effect Modder A tool used to install and manage Mass Effect textures.
Batch Install Group — A feature of M3 that installs a preconfigured list of mods.

Before We Begin

▸

Before we can get this crawl going, there are a few last things you need to know:

Version:
This guide is written for the English release of the game on Steam or the EA App.
Only Windows 10 64-bit (Non-LTSC) or newer is officially supported. It may run on Linux, but I cannot offer you help if you try it.

Legal:
It should go without saying, but cracked or modified copies are not supported.
The game goes regularly on sale for as low as 5$/€/£ No excuses, Commander.

Performance:
Generally, if you can run the LE and get a stable framerate, you'll be fine.
My setup (NVIDIA RTX 2060 Super [8 GB VRAM], Ryzen 7 3700X, 16 GB RAM) is a touch older, but runs all three games fully modded at 1080p / 60 FPS.
I also strongly recommend installing the game on an SSD to keep load times down.

Gameplay:
This guide was designed with Keyboard and Mouse in mind.
Not that controllers won't work, but I don't play that way so I can't guarantee anything.

Additionally, there are a few things you’ll need to bring to the bar table:

Time:
This guide should take roughly one hour per game. Texture modding depends heavily on your hardware, but can extend the time per game by another hour or more.

Attention:
Don’t skim please. I wrote each step down for a reason. Skipping will most likely lead to issues down the line.

Basic PC familiarity:
Installing software, handling archives, navigating file paths and light troubleshooting.

Disk space:
The Legendary Edition itself takes up ~110 GB.
Depending on your choices, having ~200 GB free should cover most setups.
Modding all three games at once and/or storing all archives can push this closer to ~250 GB.

Archive tool:
You’ll need something to open compressed files. I recommend 7-Zip.

Downloads:
All mods for the LE are hosted on Nexus Mods. You’ll need a free account to download them.



?

Caution: Free accounts are capped at 3 MB/s. Considering that some mods are quite large, a month of Premium may be worthwhile.
A few mods also offer torrent downloads as an alternative. If you choose that route, I recommend Transmission.

Backup (Optional):
If you have spare storage, I highly recommend backing up your unmodded game folder.
More on this later.
