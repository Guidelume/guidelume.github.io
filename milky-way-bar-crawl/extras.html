After Hours - Extras

Time To Choose

▾

Welcome to the Extras Section!

This is where you'll find mods that didn't quite make the main lists, but are still well worth a look.
While I can recommend each of them in good conscience, I don't personally use every mod listed here. Because of that, support is necessarily a little more limited, so be sure to read each mod page carefully before installing any of them.

A Note on Appearance Mods

!

Many appearance mods require you to use the Trilogy Save Editor to give your Shepard these styles manually.
Be extra careful reading the instructions for these mods.

LE1

▾

A light sprinkling of flavor for a glass that is threatening to shatter if you look at it funny.


UNC Morality

By: vegz
Allows you to play both UNC missions that are normally gated behind a high paragon/renegade score.

LE1 Definitive Audio Overhaul

By: Devinite
Overhauls a ton of sounds effects by using those of later games.

Replenish Grenades LE1

By: HenBagle
Allows you to replenish your grenades from your locker on the Normandy.

Quick Loot (LE1)

By: rondeeno
Prevents the recovered items menu from appearing when looting, making the experience more seamless.

Less Intense Vigil

By: HenBagle
Reduces the visual intensity of Vigil for those sensitive to flashing lights.

Children of Rannoch (LE1)

By: mithran56 and naNuke
A very extensive visual overhaul of the Quarians.
I like the vanilla look of the Quarians, but this is probably my favorite reinterpretation of them.
The only reason it's here is because, as of writing, there is no version for LE3 yet.

Smoother GUI (LE1)

By: Deepk
The menus and other GUI elements in the game are animated at 30fps which might come across as sluggish. This mod increases their fps to 60.
I've been staring at these menus for over a decade so seeing them so smooth just looks odd to me.

Omni-Key Separator (LE1)

By: Herobrine24
A lot of interactions like sprint or use are bundled into a single Omni-Key, i.e. spacebar or equivalent. This mod lets you freely rebind these actions.
I never found this particularly annoying.

Punch Charles Saracino

By: beccatoria
Does what it says on the tin.
The interaction is funny, but I don't need to see it every playthrough.

Same-Gender Romances (LE1)

By: rondeeno
Allows Ashley and Kaidan to be romanced by both MaleShep and FemShep.
I usually end up romancing Liara or Garrus, so I've never had much use for it.

Community Creators: Appearance

If you're looking to customize your Shepard, there are two creators I'd like to highlight:

MorningAngel

and

ClericOfShadows

Their work is truly without equal.
