Installation

Who say the journey can't be just as enjoyable as the destination? Here’s the secret to a good bar crawl:

  1. Open M3.
  2. Select Mod Management → Batch Mod Installer.
  3. Load the batch file:
    • If you downloaded the list through M3, it will appear automatically.
    • If you downloaded it manually, place the file into: (Your M3 folder)/mods/BatchModQueues
      Then reopen the Batch Mod Installer.

  4. Download every mod through the Batch Mod Installer whenever possible. This ensures you get the correct versions of everything.
    In cases where it is not possible to go through the Installer, simply download and import manually.
    Depending on your Nexus membership and file sizes, this may take a little while. Please be patient with your aging barman (28).
  5. Once everything has finished downloading, click Install this group.
  6. When asked if you want to use previously saved options, click Yes.
  7. Wait until everything is installed, then launch the game once to make sure everything is working.
  8. Congratulations! You've survived the easy part. Before you start playing, head on over to Finish.

Patches / Compatibility

If you were to install the mods listed below from top to bottom, all relevant patches should appear when needed.
Remember that you are shaping your own flavor of drink here however, so be sure to always read thoroughly through each mod's Nexus page.



LE1 Community Patch

By: HenBagle and the LE1 Community Patch Team
If you are only ever going to install a single mod, pick this one.
Bugfix mods are always a bit unglamorous by nature, but the work the community has put into these ever since the ME1Recalibrated days is beyond astonishing.
Note: This mod is foundational and required by basically every other mod on this list.

Myriad Pro Begone

By: Audemus
Replaces the font used throughout the game with something more fitting for the setting.
It also recolors the conversation wheel to a teal color, so each game gets its own color identity.
Note 1: You need to install this after the Community Patch for technical reasons.
Note 2: The file you download has a version for every game plus the launcher. You may import everything if you want or just choose the LE1 version.

Advanced Weapon Models

By: Knighthawk1895
Replaces the weapon models with their updated counterparts from the later games.
Note: I strongly recommend you choose the "Full" option during installation.

Halcyon Hairpack

By: Audemus
Adds over a dozen new hairstyles for NPCs based on designs from The Outer Worlds.
Note: To apply these new hairstyles to your Shepard you will need to edit your save file. More on that in the Textures section.

Appearance Modification Menu

By: DropTheSquid
Allows you to override the casual and combat appearance for yourself and your companions.
Think of it like a Transmog system.

Geth Retrofits

By: Audemus
Overhauls and diversifies the Geth enemies you encounter to bring them in line with LE3.
Note: This mod requires Appearance Modification Menu.

Streamlined Weapon Loadouts

By: Knighthawk1895
Limits the weapons each character can equip to those their class is proficient in, similarly to how it works in LE2 and LE3.

Elements of Comparative Weaponry

By: 55tumbl
More accurate and relevant weapon stats for easier comparison.
Believe me, once you notice how non-descript the vanilla stats really are, you will never want to play without it.

Pinnacle Station DLC

By: Mgamerz
Restores the DLC that was lost in the transition to the LE.
An amazing effort by the community, though I am not sure how many people actually clamored to play Pinnacle Station again.
It's not a bad DLC just pretty basic. The authors added some cool QoL features though, check them out.

Private Message Terminal

By: 55tumbl
Adds a message terminal to the CIC and Shepard's cabin, similar to the ones in LE2 or LE3.
Small addition, big impact, in my opinion.

A Little Help From My Friends

By: 55tumbl
Receive messages from your friends on the Normandy to help you with the collection assignments.
Note: This mod requires Private Message Terminal.

ME1 Dossiers Restored

By: Cirosan
Restores cut messages and dossiers from the long defunct ME1 website.
Note: This mod requires Private Message Terminal.

Casual Hubs

By: rondeeno
You and your squadmates wear casual outfits during Hub visits (e.g. Citadel) instead of armor.

Black Market License

By: 55tumbl
Restores powerful, cut gear for you to find all over the galaxy.
Note: You may find these additions overpowered. Thankfully you can remove them from the loot pool again through the same store you get the titular license from.

Return to Normandy Button

By: vegz
During sidequests this will allow you to return to the Normandy from the map screen instead of having to walk back to the airlock.

Normandy Rapid Transit

By: rondeeno
Allows you to return to the Normandy through the Rapid Transit terminals instead of having to endure twenty elevators.

Skip Minigames

By: 55tumbl
This mod doesn't so much remove the minigames as make bypassing them cost 0 Omni-Gel, so if you really wanted to, you could still play them.
Let's be honest though: the thrill of playing cylindrical Frogger (among other things) to open every container wears thin pretty quickly.
There are a small handful of exceptions where it makes narrative sense to play them manually, like the Towers of Hanoi puzzle on Peak 15.

Morlan's Iconic Armor Store

By: Kinkojiro
Restores Morlan selling iconic armor for your squadmates, like the ones you see them wearing in promotional art or trailers.

Charted Worlds

By: 55tumbl
Unveils Points of Interest on the Uncharted World maps.
This is such an unbelievable QoL improvement it baffles me we needed a mod for it.

Galaxy Map Trackers

By: 55tumbl
Tracks active and undiscovered assignments on the galaxy map.
No more jumping to random star systems to see if Hackett pings you about your spaceship's extended warranty.

Mission Timings

By: rondeeno
Virmire now won't unlock until you've done the other three main missions.
A small adjustment but I think it makes the story flow better.

N7 Mako

By: 55tumbl
Adjusts the handling of the Mako ground vehicle.
Slightly anyway. Personally I've always been a Mako apologist, but this mod does improve the general feel of driving.

XP Rescale

By: 55tumbl
Rebalances XP gains to make reaching max level in one playthrough possible.
I recommend you use the "Classic" 1-60 leveling in-game when using this mod.
Note: I recommend the default install option.

Dr. Heart Experiments

By: Marcus22Khaar
Changes the appearance of a special enemy type to better match the quest.

Saren Stages

By: CATACHR1SM
Changes Saren's appearance throughout the game as he becomes ever more indoctrinated.
Note: This mod offers a lot of options through the in-game Mod Settings menu.
Personally I enjoy "Robes/Less Implants/Yellow Eyes" during the Prologue and the Citadel hearing, and "Armor/Less Implants" on Virmire.

Alliance Uniform Consistency

By: Mistyvail
Updates Alliance uniforms to bring them visually in line with how they look in LE3.

Liara Consistency Mod

By: Marcus22Khaar
Updates Liara's design to make it consistent across games.
Did you know that she has four(!) different models throughout the trilogy?
Note 1: You have two options here. Personally I recommend her LE3 appearance to have her be consistent throughout the trilogy.
Note 2: If you plan on following the Textures section later, the LE3 version is required.

Anderson Consistency Mod

By: Marcus22Khaar
Backports Anderson's LE3 appearance for consistency across the trilogy.

Prothean Consistency Mod

By: vegz
Changes Vigil's appearance so that a faint Prothean silhouette can be seen amidst the noise.

Unique Udina Face Restored LE1

By: Marcus22Khaar
Backports a unique look for Udina that was cut from LE3 and later reused for Bryson from the Leviathan DLC.
Takes a little getting used to but honestly it suits him.

MELLO

By: CATACHR1SM
Completely restores the dynamic lighting that was lost in the transition to the LE.
Screenshots don't do this one justice, it really makes a tremendous difference.
Note: I recommend choosing the default option even if you plan on having some effects disabled.

LE1 Diversification Project

By: Audemus
A massive overhaul of every part of the game with the goal of making the world feel more lived in.
Honestly though this is I think my favorite mod for LE1. It would take me ages to describe why or even scratch the surface of all that it does, so definitely check out the Nexus page.
Note: This mod requires Advanced Weapon Models (Full) and Halcyon Hairpack.

LE1 Datapads Collection

By: MajorKirrahe89
Adds around 80 new, lore-friendly datapads all over the game which breathe more life into the universe by highlighting the daily lives of its people.

Spectre Expansion Mod - LE1

By: Audemus
An expanded Galaxy Map, new lore and additional content.
Note: LE1 Diversification Project is a soft requirement, but needed to access the new Hanar Embassy.

Remove Feros Color Filter

By: Herobrine24
The LE added a yellow filter to Feros that this mod removes.
Personal preference, really. It just reminds me way too much of those seventh-gen filters.

Keepers Finders

By: 55tumbl
Marks the location of all Keepers on your map for the relevant quest.

A Lot Of Videos

By: Audemus and ALOV Team
Upscales every pre-rendered scene in the trilogy to 4K 60FPS.
A monumental effort involving scene-by-scene restortation, artifact cleanup, modern Bink 2 encoding, and even recreating some cinematics in-engine.
